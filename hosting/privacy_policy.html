Selene – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains how Prince Kumar ("we", "us") handles your information when you use the Selene mobile app ("Selene"). Contact: princekumarmandal78@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

DataWhy
Account: user ID, and email address if you sign in with email (guest mode needs no email)Create and secure your account

We do not collect your precise location, contacts or payment card details. Location metadata (EXIF) is removed from palm photos on your device before upload.

2. How AI is used

Readings are generated by Google's Gemini models, which we call from our own servers (Google Cloud Functions). Your photo, question or message and the relevant profile details (such as first name, zodiac sign and language) are sent to Gemini only to create your response. We use Google's paid API terms, under which Google does not use this content to train its models.

3. Palm photos

  • By default your palm photo is deleted from our servers as soon as your reading is created. A compressed copy stays on your device so the line overlay can be shown.
  • Photos that are not recognised as a palm are deleted immediately.
  • If you turn on "Keep palm photos" in Profile, photos are stored privately and can be deleted at any time with "Delete palm photos".

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We use Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check and Gemini API) and RevenueCat (subscription management) as service providers to run the app. Payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card details. Data is stored in Google Cloud in India (asia-south1). We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data until you delete it. In the app go to Profile → Delete my account & data to permanently erase your profile, readings, chats and photos, or see how to delete your account. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

6. Security

All traffic is encrypted with TLS. Email accounts must verify their address before use. Access to our backend is restricted to our app through Firebase App Check and per-user security rules.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR or India's DPDP Act 2023), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to withdraw consent. Email princekumarmandal78@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

8. Children

Selene is not for children. You must be at least 13 (16 in the EEA) to use it. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Entertainment only

Readings are for entertainment and self-reflection. They are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

10. Changes

If we update this policy we will change the date above and tell you in the app when the changes are significant.
